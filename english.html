Shifen Old Street & Waterfall

Exploring a lively place full of nature, culture, and memories

My Shifen Experience

Shifen was so lively and pretty. We walked along the streets, saw many lanterns, and enjoyed the aesthetic atmosphere of both nature and people. The old street was full of unique shops selling souvenirs and snacks. I bought a few cute things to bring home.

At the Shifen Waterfall, I was amazed by the beautiful view. It felt like something from a movie. My favorite part was writing my wishes on the lantern and releasing it into the sky with my friends.

This place was filled with nature, memories, and photo spots. I felt so happy and peaceful after the visit. It’s the kind of place I’d recommend to friends who love nature and taking aesthetic pictures.

Trip Questions & Reflections

  • When did you feel the most excited during your visit? When I launched the lantern into the sky.
  • Where did you find the best street food or souvenirs? At the stalls near the train tracks.
  • Did the place look better on social media? It was actually more beautiful in real life!
  • How was the temperature there? It was cool and fresh, especially near the waterfall.
  • What was your favorite moment there? Taking pictures with my friends by the waterfall.
  • Who would you recommend this trip to? Friends who love photography, culture, and food.
  • What things did you buy there? Keychains, snacks, postcards, and a mini lantern.
  • How was the overall experience there? Unforgettable! I would love to go back again.
  • Where is the best spot to take pictures in the Shifen Waterfall? From the wooden platform facing the full waterfall view.
  • How did you feel after visiting? Grateful, relaxed, and very happy.
